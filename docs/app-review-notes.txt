3. FUNCTION AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Doner Kebab Map helps users quickly find doner, kebab, and gyro restaurants near them. General map apps mix all restaurants together, which makes this type of food slow to find, especially when travelling. The app shows a focused map of nearby places with distance, estimated driving/walking time, arrival time, address, and one-tap directions in Apple Maps or Google Maps. Users can save favorites. Audience: general public (4+). Free, no ads, no in-app purchases.

4. SETUP AND ACCESS
No credentials or sample files needed.
- Launch the app; a short onboarding appears (Next / Skip).
- Allow location ("Allow While Using App"). Only When-In-Use location is requested.
- Nearby places load automatically as pins.
- Tap a pin for details: distance, travel time, arrival time, address, phone/website when available, favorite (heart), Get Directions, Share.
- List tab: same places sorted by distance.
- More tab: favorites (swipe to delete), appearance (System/Light/Dark), version, contact.
If location is denied, the app still opens and offers a button to open Settings.

5. EXTERNAL SERVICES
Only Apple frameworks: MapKit (place search and travel-time estimates), Core Location (device location), MessageUI (contact email, user-initiated). Directions open Apple Maps or Google Maps at the user's choice; only destination coordinates are passed in the URL. No backend server, no authentication, no payments, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no AI services. Favorites and settings are stored locally on the device only. No personal data is collected or shared (see privacy manifest and privacy policy).

6. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in all regions; no region-locked features or content. The UI is localized in English, German, and French. The only variation is the number of results, which depends on Apple Maps coverage in the area.

7. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Place data comes from Apple MapKit via public APIs. The app icon, logo, and artwork are original.
