Doner Kebab Map — find the best döner, kebab & gyro spots near you.

How it works

Open the app and it shows döner, kebab and gyro places around you on a map and in a distance-sorted list. Tap a place to see details, get directions with Apple or Google Maps, and save it to your favorites.

No places are showing up.

Make sure location access is enabled (Settings → Doner Kebab Map → Location) and that you have an internet connection. Then reopen the app or tap “Try Again”.

How do I get directions?

Tap a place, then “Get Directions” and choose Apple Maps or Google Maps.

How do I save a favorite?

Open a place and tap the heart icon. Your favorites appear in the “More” tab.

Is my data collected?

No. The app has no account and no server. Your location, favorites and settings stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone running iOS 17 or later.
